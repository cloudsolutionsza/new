About the book

What if consciousness came before existence?

Before there was a universe, there was nothing.
Not darkness.
Not silence.
Not empty space.
Nothing.

And within that nothing, something became aware.

Sophia does not know where she came from. She has no body, no memory, no world to stand in—only consciousness suspended in an impossible absence. At first, she believes she is alone.

Then she discovers a boundary.

Something exists beyond what she can perceive. Something that should not be there.

The closer Sophia gets to it, the more reality begins to change. What starts as a question about existence becomes a terrifying discovery: the universe may not have begun with matter, energy, or even time. It may have begun with an act of consciousness.

But if consciousness existed before the universe, who—or what—was conscious?

And if Sophia can reach beyond the boundary, she may discover that she is part of creation.

She may be part of the reason it exists.

Nothingless is a philosophical science-fiction novel about consciousness, creation, free will, and the dangerous space between what exists and what should be possible.

For readers who enjoy cosmic science fiction, metaphysical mysteries, reality-bending ideas, and stories that leave you questioning the nature of existence long after the final page.
